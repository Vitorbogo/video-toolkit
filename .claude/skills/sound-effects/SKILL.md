# Sound effects

The library lives in `sfx/` (in git) and is generated by synthesis: `python3 src/vtk/sfx.py generate`. To change a
sound, change its recipe in `src/vtk/sfx.py` and generate again. Don't download files from websites: the repo is
public, and only generated or CC0 sound may go in.

## Sounds (approved by the author on 2026-11-05)

| Sound | When to use |
|-------|-------------|
| `whoosh-short` | transition: change of topic or slide |
| `pop-wood` | punchline, a turn ("and that's not true") |
| `pop-bubble` | idea or text appearing |
| `pop-blip` | list item, number |
| `click` | on-screen action: clicking, sending the prompt |

Rejected by the author: the long whoosh and the pop that dropped in pitch ("didn't like it"). The author prefers
short, subtle effects.

## How to decide

1. `python3 src/vtk/sfx.py marks --plan videos/<name>/plan.json` lists the start of each segment and of each
   caption, already in final video time.
2. Pick few moments. Think one effect every 8 to 15 s, never less than 3.5 s between two (`check` rejects it).
   Don't put a whoosh on every cut: only where the topic changes. Never an effect over the hook in the first 2 s
   if it covers the speech.
3. Write `videos/<name>/sfx.json`: `[{"t": 11.05, "sound": "whoosh-short"}, ...]`. `t` is the moment the sound
   hits (for the whoosh, the cut). `vol` is optional; the catalog default already sits under the voice.
4. `python3 src/vtk/sfx.py check --sfx videos/<name>/sfx.json --plan videos/<name>/plan.json`.
5. Show the list to the author together with the cuts review, with the sentence for each moment. They approve or
   remove.
6. At the audio step: `postaudio.py ... --sfx videos/<name>/sfx.json`. The effects are mixed in before the audio chain
   (they go through the gentle 2:1 compressor with the voice, and then loudnorm), so the result stays at -14 LUFS.
