# video-toolkit

Local tools and agent skills that turn one person's screen-and-camera recording into a short vertical video ready to publish. Anyone can clone it and use it for their own videos.

## Language

**Author**:
The person who records, reviews and publishes the videos. Each clone of the toolkit serves one author.
_Avoid_: user, creator

**Preference**:
A choice that is right for one author and could be wrong for another: how their voice is treated, which effects they like, their look, their habits on camera.
_Avoid_: taste, setting, style (alone)

**Craft rule**:
Something that holds for any author: a technical limit, a platform rule, or a lesson about what fails no matter who is recording.
_Avoid_: best practice, preference

**Default**:
What the toolkit does for an author who has not decided a preference: the neutral choice, such as the voice left untouched.
_Avoid_: standard, fallback

## Author profile

**Author profile**:
The private record of one author's preferences, split into four parts: voice, brand, channel and recording.
_Avoid_: settings, config, personal context

**Voice profile**:
How the author's voice is treated, and the mic and room facts behind it.

**Brand profile**:
How the author's videos look and sound: colors, captions, illustrations, sound effects, covers.

**Channel profile**:
Who the author's videos are for and how they talk: audience, tone, hashtags, calls to action.

**Recording profile**:
The author's setup and habits on camera, and where their recordings and finished files live.

**Status**:
Where a preference stands: decided, testing (still being compared across videos) or rejected (heard and turned down, never proposed again). A setup fact has no status, only when it was seen.

**Spoken language**:
The language a video is spoken in, detected per video from its audio. A property of the toolkit, not a preference.
_Avoid_: channel language
